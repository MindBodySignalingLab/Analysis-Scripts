Angle codebook
Format: angle number = (point_a, vertex_point, point_c)
The middle point is the angle vertex.

angle_1 = (11, 13, 15)
angle_2 = (12, 14, 16)
angle_3 = (11, 13, 23)
angle_4 = (12, 14, 24)
angle_5 = (11, 23, 25)
angle_6 = (12, 24, 26)

Vertex speed variables
vertex_speed_angle_1_p13_coord_per_s = speed of p13, the vertex joint of angle_1
vertex_speed_angle_2_p14_coord_per_s = speed of p14, the vertex joint of angle_2
vertex_speed_angle_3_p13_coord_per_s = speed of p13, the vertex joint of angle_3
vertex_speed_angle_4_p14_coord_per_s = speed of p14, the vertex joint of angle_4
vertex_speed_angle_5_p23_coord_per_s = speed of p23, the vertex joint of angle_5
vertex_speed_angle_6_p24_coord_per_s = speed of p24, the vertex joint of angle_6

Symmetry variables
symmetry_absdiff_angle_1_vs_2_deg = abs(angle_1 - angle_2)
symmetry_absdiff_angle_3_vs_4_deg = abs(angle_3 - angle_4)
symmetry_absdiff_angle_5_vs_6_deg = abs(angle_5 - angle_6)
